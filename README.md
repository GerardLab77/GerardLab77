# Hi, I'm Gerard

I'm a Computer Science with AI student at the University of Sheffield. I’m particularly interested in machine learning, data engineering and understanding why models behave the way they do.

Most of my projects start with a question I genuinely want answered. Recently, that has meant investigating shortcut learning in image classifiers, analysing competitive Yu-Gi-Oh! decklists and experimenting with classical and quantum optimisation methods.

## Selected projects

* **[Waterbirds Training Dynamics](https://github.com/GerardLab77/waterbirds-training-dynamics)** — Research into whether early training signals can identify shortcut-conflicting examples, achieving roughly 0.88 ROC-AUC by epoch five.
* **[YGO MetaPulse](https://github.com/GerardLab77/ygo-metapulse)** — A Python, SQLite and Streamlit pipeline for turning Yu-Gi-Oh! decklists into interactive metagame analysis.
* **[Quantum Portfolio Optimiser](https://github.com/GerardLab77/quantum-solver)** — A comparison of brute force, greedy search, simulated annealing and QAOA for constrained portfolio selection.

I mainly work with Python, PyTorch, pandas, SQL and Streamlit. Away from my computer science work, I enjoy making art, going to the gym, playing Yu-Gi-Oh! and League of Legends, and watching basketball.
